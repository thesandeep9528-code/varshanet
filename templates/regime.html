Standard Meteorological Regimes

🌧️ Active Monsoon

Description: Characterized by strong cross-equatorial flow, low MSLP, and high moisture transport. The monsoon trough lies in its normal position across central India.

Typical Rainfall: Widespread moderate to heavy rainfall over Central India and Western Ghats (30–100+ mm/day).

Affected Regions: Central India, Western Ghats, Indo-Gangetic plains.

🌤️ Break Monsoon

Description: Shift of the monsoon trough to the Himalayan foothills. Weak winds over the peninsula with positive OLR anomalies indicating suppressed convection.

Typical Rainfall: Decreased rainfall over main landmass (<10 mm/day), enhanced rainfall over NE India and foothills.

Affected Regions: NE India, sub-Himalayan foothills (enhanced); Central India (suppressed).

🌀 Monsoon Depression / Low

Description: Intense low-pressure systems typically originating in the Bay of Bengal, with strong vorticity and deep convection, moving westward/northwestward.

Typical Rainfall: Extremely heavy rainfall along its track (100–300+ mm/day). High risk of flooding.

Affected Regions: Coastal Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra along the track.

⛰️ Orographic Rainfall

Description: Enhanced rainfall due to forced ascent of moist air over Western Ghats, NE hills, and sub-Himalayan regions. Strong westerly/southwesterly low-level jet present.

Typical Rainfall: Very heavy rainfall on windward slopes (50–200+ mm/day), rain shadow on leeward side.

Affected Regions: Western Ghats (Kerala, Goa, coastal Karnataka), NE India hills, Meghalaya plateau.

🌊 Coastal Rainfall

Description: Coastal convergence events driven by sea-breeze interaction, land-sea temperature gradients, and onshore flow. Often mesoscale in nature.

Typical Rainfall: Moderate to heavy rainfall concentrated near coastline (30–100 mm/day).

Affected Regions: Tamil Nadu coast, Konkan, Malabar coast, Odisha-Andhra coast.

❄️ Western Disturbance

Description: Extratropical synoptic-scale systems originating in the Mediterranean that bring winter and pre-monsoon precipitation to northern India.

Typical Rainfall: Moderate rainfall and snowfall over NW India (10–60 mm/day).

Affected Regions: Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Punjab, NW Rajasthan.
