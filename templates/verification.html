Key Findings

  • VarshaNet 2.0 demonstrates consistent improvement across all rainfall thresholds compared to raw NWP output.
  • The most significant improvement is observed for heavy rainfall events (>64.5 mm), where ETS improved by 87% and POD improved by 73%.
  • False Alarm Ratio for heavy rainfall reduced from 0.65 to 0.45, a 31% reduction, enabling more reliable warnings.
  • Regime-aware correction is particularly effective during Monsoon Depression events, where raw NWP models typically underpredict extreme rainfall.
  • The Fractions Skill Score (FSS) shows that spatial accuracy at neighbourhood scales of 25km and above meets the useful skill threshold (FSS>0.5) for corrected forecasts.
