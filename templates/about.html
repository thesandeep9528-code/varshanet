About VarshaNet 2.0

VarshaNet 2.0 is a flagship initiative by the National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting (NCMRWF) under the Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES), Government of India. The primary objective is to bridge the gap between global-scale Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) model outputs and actionable, high-resolution district-level forecasts.

By incorporating advanced regime-aware machine learning techniques, the system dynamically recognizes synoptic weather patterns (regimes) and applies targeted statistical bias corrections. This approach significantly reduces systematic model errors, particularly in predicting heavy rainfall events during the Indian Summer Monsoon (June–September).

Mission Statement: To improve the accuracy and reliability of monsoon rainfall forecasts at district level, enabling timely and effective disaster preparedness and water resource management across India.

Methodology Details

🌀 Regime Classification

A Random Forest classifier trained on 3000+ labeled meteorological samples identifies 6 distinct monsoon regimes using key variables: MSLP anomaly, 850hPa wind speed, OLR anomaly, moisture flux, and lower-level vorticity.

Accuracy: ~85% on validation data

📊 Bias Correction

Regime-specific statistical corrections including quantile mapping (Active), multiplicative correction (Break), non-linear scaling (Depression), elevation-dependent enhancement (Orographic), wind-direction correction (Coastal), and temperature-based adjustment (Western Disturbance).

⛈️ Heavy Rainfall Probability

Gamma distribution-based probabilistic framework estimates the likelihood of rainfall exceeding IMD operational thresholds (64.5 mm for Heavy, 115.6 mm for Very Heavy, 204.5 mm for Extremely Heavy).

✅ Verification Framework

Comprehensive verification using WMO-standard metrics including RMSE, ETS, CSI, POD, FAR, Bias Score, and Fractions Skill Score (FSS) computed against IMD gridded rainfall observations.
